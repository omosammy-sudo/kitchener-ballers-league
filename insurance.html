Insurance

Every player needs valid cover before playing

Why Insurance Matters

We strongly recommend that all players, particularly recent graduates who are transitioning to OHIP coverage, obtain temporary medical insurance to protect themselves against unexpected medical expenses.

Make sure your policy covers recreational sports injuries, ambulance services, and emergency medical treatment. Without it, surgery and medical care after an injury can be very expensive.

What You Need

  • Valid personal accident or sports insurance for the current season.
  • Coverage that includes 6-aside or indoor football.
  • Your name on the policy, matching the name you register under.

Confirm the minimum coverage and approved providers with your insurer before publishing.

How to Submit Proof

  1. Buy or renew your insurance.
  2. Save a PDF or a clear photo of your policy or certificate.
  3. Upload it using the form below.
